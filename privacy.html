ZIREX Privacy Policy

ZIREX is an academic research prototype that runs locally on the user's own computer.

  • ZIREX requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly) only after the account owner gives consent.
  • ZIREX never sends, modifies, moves or deletes emails.
  • Emails are analyzed on the local computer to detect phishing. Data is not sold or shared.
  • Only minimal, anonymized analysis findings may be sent to Google's Gemini API, and only with the user's explicit consent.
  • Users can disconnect Gmail at any time inside ZIREX, which deletes the stored access token.
  • Use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
